02 The 7pm draw

How you know the draw is straight.

The draw is committed before it happens. At 6:30pm, when number entries close, the draw system locks in a sealed result and publishes its fingerprint (a cryptographic hash) right here on this page. At 7:00pm, on the big screen in the bar, the seal is opened live: the 7 numbers are revealed, and anyone can check that they match the fingerprint published half an hour earlier.

What that means in plain terms: the bar cannot change the numbers after seeing your entries — and you can verify it yourself, every week, from your own phone.
