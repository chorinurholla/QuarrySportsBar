Pay at the bar. Pick on your phone. Win in the room.

  1. Spend ₦5,000 or more at the bar — your waiter gives you your entry code. One entry per person, every Saturday.
  2. Enter your code on this site and make your picks: the winner of every match on the card, plus your 7 lucky numbers.
