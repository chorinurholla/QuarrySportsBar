01 The walkthrough

A Saturday at Quarry, start to finish.

Thursday. The card is published — every Saturday Premier League match, plus 3 Bundesliga and 3 La Liga games — on this site and on our screens.

Saturday, before first kickoff. Spend ₦5,000 or more at the bar and your waiter gives you your entry code — the entry itself is free; it comes with your normal spend. On your phone, enter the code at Play, pick Home/Draw/Away for every match, add your total-goals tie-break, and choose your 7 numbers from 0–49. Submit before the first match kicks off — numbers stay open until 6:30pm.

Saturday afternoon. The matches play. Standings run live on the site and the screens.

7:00pm. The bingo draw runs live on the big screen — numbers one at a time until somebody completes all 7. The entire drawing order is committed before entries close, so nobody (including us) can steer it. How that works.

After the last whistle. The Match Picks winner is announced and paid the same night. Everything is published on the Winners page.

02 The money

Every naira, exactly.

Match Picks Challenge

Most correct picks wins ₦50,000 — paid as ₦20,000 cash + ₦30,000 bar & kitchen credit, the same night.

Ties

First tie-break: closest total-goals prediction. Still tied? The prize is shared equally between the tied players.

7 Lucky Numbers — bingo

From 7:00pm the draw runs live on the big screen: numbers from 0–49, drawn one at a time. The first entry to have all 7 of its numbers drawn wins ₦20,000 bar & kitchen credit — a guaranteed winner every match day. If two or more entries complete on the same ball, they share equally. The winner is determined by the sealed drawing order, not by who shouts first — shouting BINGO is tradition, the machine is the referee.

The bingo prize is independent of the Match Picks pool — one never shrinks the other. The full drawing order is sealed before numbers close and verifiable afterwards on the Winners page.

Minimum entries

The full ₦50,000 Match Picks pool is guaranteed when at least 25 entries are in. On a quieter week, the pool is ₦2,000 multiplied by the number of entries instead — stated here and on your slip, so there are never surprises after the fact. Quiet weeks are published on the Winners page with the same honesty as big ones.

Claiming

Same night at the bar, or within 14 days with your entry code and ID. Bar credit is valid for 30 days, dine-in only.

03 Who can play

Eligibility and fair play.

  • 18+ only. ID may be checked.
  • One entry per person per Saturday. Codes are single-use and valid only for the Saturday they're bought.
  • Quarry staff and their immediate family are not eligible to win.
  • No edits after submission — the same rule for everyone.
  • Management may refuse service in line with venue policy.
  • By entering, you agree we may publish your first name and photo if you win. You can decline the photo and still be paid.

04 Edge cases

When football gets in the way.

  • A match is postponed or abandoned: that fixture is void for everyone — the week is scored from the remaining fixtures.
  • Fewer than 8 fixtures complete: the week is void and codes roll to the next Saturday.
  • The site goes down before cut-off: paper entries at the bar, same cut-offs.
  • The draw: the draw shown on the big screen is definitive; the site mirrors it.
  • Any other dispute: raise it the same night or the next day with your code. Management's decision is final.

05 FAQ

The awkward questions, answered.

Is this gambling?

Entry comes free with a minimum ₦5,000 bar spend — you're buying food and drinks, and the game comes with it. The Match Picks game is a skill contest and the competition runs as a venue promotion. [Wording pending legal review]

Do I have to stay at the bar all day?

No. Once you have your code you can submit from anywhere before kickoff. But the draw at 7:00pm and the payout happen live in the room — that's the good part.

What if only 10 people enter?

The pool becomes ₦2,000 per entry instead of the full ₦50,000 — see minimum entries. And your entry cost you nothing beyond the food and drinks you were buying anyway. It's stated before you enter, never announced after.

Can I change my picks after submitting?

No — no edits after submission, for anyone. Check the read-back screen before you hit submit.

What if I lose my receipt?

Staff can look up your code by your phone number at the bar.

How do I know the draw isn't fixed?

The result is cryptographically sealed and its fingerprint published before number entries close; the reveal at 7:00pm must match it. Full explanation.

Do you keep my phone number?

We use it only to contact you about your entry and — only if you opt in — to send results and the weekly card on WhatsApp. [Privacy wording pending legal review]

Can I enter for my friend?

One entry per person — bring them along instead.
