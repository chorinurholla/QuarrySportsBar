01 The walkthrough

A Saturday at Quarry, start to finish.

Thursday. The card is published — every Saturday Premier League match, plus 3 Bundesliga and 3 La Liga games — on this site and on our screens.

Saturday, before first kickoff. Pay ₦5,000 at the bar and your entry code prints on your receipt (drink included [offer to be confirmed]). On your phone, enter the code at Play, pick Home/Draw/Away for every match, add your total-goals tie-break, and choose your 7 numbers from 0–49. Submit before the first match kicks off — numbers stay open until 9:00pm.

Saturday afternoon. The matches play. Standings run live on the site and the screens.

9:30pm. The numbers are drawn live on the big screen — the draw is committed before entries close, so nobody (including us) can steer it. How that works.

After the last whistle. The Match Picks winner is announced and paid the same night. Everything is published on the Winners page.

03 Who can play

Eligibility and fair play.

  • 18+ only. ID may be checked.
  • One entry per person per Saturday. Codes are single-use and valid only for the Saturday they're bought.
  • Quarry staff and their immediate family are not eligible to win.
  • No edits after submission — the same rule for everyone.
  • Management may refuse service in line with venue policy; entry fees for refused service are refunded [policy to be confirmed].
  • By entering, you agree we may publish your first name and photo if you win. You can decline the photo and still be paid.

04 Edge cases

When football gets in the way.

  • A match is postponed or abandoned: that fixture is void for everyone — the week is scored from the remaining fixtures.
  • Fewer than [8] fixtures complete [to be confirmed]: the week is void and codes roll to the next Saturday.
  • The site goes down before cut-off: paper entries at the bar, same cut-offs.
  • The draw: the draw shown on the big screen is definitive; the site mirrors it.
  • Any other dispute: raise it the same night or the next day with your code. Management's decision is final.

05 FAQ

The awkward questions, answered.

Is this gambling?

Entry is part of a bar purchase, the Match Picks game is a skill contest, and the competition runs as a venue promotion — you're buying a night out that comes with a game. [Wording pending legal review]

Do I have to stay at the bar all day?

No. Once you have your code you can submit from anywhere before kickoff. But the draw at 9:30 and the payout happen live in the room — that's the good part.

What if only 10 people enter?

The pool becomes 80% of that week's entry fees instead of the full ₦100,000 — see minimum entries. It's stated on your slip before you enter, never announced after.

Can I change my picks after submitting?

No — no edits after submission, for anyone. Check the read-back screen before you hit submit.

What if I lose my receipt?

Staff can look up your code by your phone number at the bar.

How do I know the draw isn't fixed?

The result is cryptographically sealed and its fingerprint published before number entries close; the reveal at 9:30 must match it. Full explanation.

Do you keep my phone number?

We use it only to contact you about your entry and — only if you opt in — to send results and the weekly card on WhatsApp. [Privacy wording pending legal review]

Can I enter for my friend?

One entry per person — bring them along instead.
