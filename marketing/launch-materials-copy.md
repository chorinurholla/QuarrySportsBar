## A2 Poster (walls, entrance, behind the bar)

**Top tag:** EVERY SATURDAY · LIVE AT QUARRY
**Headline:** SPEND ₦5,000. / WIN ₦50,000. / PLAY FREE.
**Sub:** Spend ₦5,000 at the bar and your entry is FREE — pick your football winners, most correct picks takes ₦50,000. Paid the same night.
**Steps:**
1. Spend ₦5,000 on food & drinks — your waiter gives you a code.
2. Pick on your phone — every match on the card, plus 7 lucky numbers.
3. In before kickoff. Winner announced and paid the same night.
**Numbers strip:** 7 LUCKY NUMBERS — drawn LIVE 7:00pm. Match 5, 6 or 7 → win up to ₦20,000. (Match 5 · ₦5,000 credit / Match 6 · ₦10,000 credit / Match all 7 · ₦20,000 credit)
**QR block:** Scan to play · quarrysportsbar.com/play · Ask your waiter for your code
**Legal line:** 18+ only · One entry per person · Entries close at first kickoff (numbers 6:30pm) · Winners paid same night · Quarry Sports Bar, inside Quarry Imperial Hotel, 52 Quarry Road, Abeokuta

## A6 Waiter Card (one per staff member, pocket-sized, two-sided)

**FRONT — The pitch (say this to every table):**
- English: "You've spent ₦5,000 — that means you get a **free entry** to win **₦50,000** this Saturday. Want me to show you? It takes two minutes."
- Pidgin: "Oga/Madam, you don spend pass five thousand — you get **free entry** to win **fifty thousand** this Saturday. Make I show you? Na two minutes."

Then help them play: (1) give a code from today's list → (2) phone to quarrysportsbar.com/play or scan the table QR → (3) code + first name + phone → pick every match → 7 numbers → Submit → (4) "Screenshot your slip" + tap "Save my slip on WhatsApp."

Footer: One code per customer per Saturday · Codes valid today only · Picks close at first kickoff · Numbers close 6:30pm · Draw 7:00pm

**BACK — Quick answers:**
- "Is it real? Who don win?" → Winners are paid the same night, in cash, in front of everyone — photos on the Winners page. First winner: launch night.
- "Wetin e cost?" → Nothing extra. Spend ₦5,000 on food and drinks, the entry is free.
- "Can I enter twice?" → One entry per person per Saturday. Bring your friends instead.
- "My code no work." → Check the digits carefully. Still failing? Bring it to the bar.
- "I lost my slip/receipt." → No wahala — the bar can look up your entry with your phone number.
- "When do I have to enter?" → Match picks close at first kickoff. Lucky numbers close 6:30pm. Draw 7:00pm on the big screen.
- "How do I collect if I win?" → Same night at the bar, or within 14 days with your code and ID.
- If you're not sure — ask the manager, don't guess. 18+ only.

## Print instructions
- Poster: A2 (420×594mm), matte or satin, full colour. Print 3–5: entrance, behind the bar, near the screens, hotel lobby.
- Table tent: A5 (148×210mm) on thick card (300gsm), one per table. (Fold-tent version: print two copies back-to-back or ask the printer to impose as a tent.)
- Waiter card: A6 (105×148mm) double-sided on card, laminated if possible — one per staff member.
- All PDFs are print-ready with the brand colours (Deep Amber #FFB300 / ink #141414 / off-white #F3F3F3). Ask the printer to print as-is, no colour "corrections".
- ⚠️ Legal note: get the lawyer's nod on the poster/tent wording at the same time as the site rules — it's the same claims in print.
